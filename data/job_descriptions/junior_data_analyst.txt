JOB DESCRIPTION: JUNIOR DATA ANALYST

Position Title: Junior Data Analyst
Department: Data & Analytics
Experience Level: Entry-Level / Junior (0–2 Years)
Employment Type: Full-Time
Location: Hybrid / Remote

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ROLE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We are seeking an enthusiastic, analytical, and detail-oriented Junior Data Analyst
to join our growing analytics team. In this role, you will work closely with data
engineers and business stakeholders to transform raw data into reliable, actionable
insights. You will be responsible for extracting data from relational databases,
performing data cleaning and normalization, conducting exploratory data analysis,
and supporting reporting needs through statistical analysis and visualization.

This is an entry-level position designed for individuals with strong foundational
skills in Python and SQL who are eager to expand their practical experience in data
manipulation, pipeline automation, and business intelligence.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Extract, inspect, and validate structured datasets from relational databases using SQL.
- Develop and maintain Python scripts utilizing Pandas for data cleaning, transformation,
  and tabular data wrangling.
- Identify data anomalies, handle missing values, eliminate duplicates, and standardize
  data types across diverse data sources.
- Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) to uncover operational trends, user behavioral
  patterns, and performance bottlenecks.
- Apply basic statistics (measures of central tendency, variance, distributions, and
  correlation analysis) to substantiate analytical observations.
- Collaborate with stakeholders to design and deliver recurring reports, analytical summaries,
  and data dashboards.
- Document analytical methodologies, maintain data dictionaries, and ensure data integrity.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS & TECHNICAL SKILLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Candidates must demonstrate competence in the following core areas:

1. Python: Strong foundation in Python programming for data analysis, scripting,
   and file handling.
2. SQL: Demonstrated ability to write and optimize relational database queries,
   multi-table JOINs, GROUP BY aggregations, and subqueries (e.g., PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite).
3. Pandas: Hands-on experience with Pandas Series and DataFrames, indexing, filtering,
   grouping, reshaping, and merging tabular data.
4. Data Cleaning: Practical knowledge of data preprocessing techniques, handling missing
   values, outlier detection, string normalization, and data validation.
5. Data Analysis: Ability to perform exploratory data analysis, interpret quantitative metrics,
   and synthesize findings into clear takeaways.
6. Basic Statistics: Sound understanding of descriptive statistics, probability distributions,
   statistical significance, and hypothesis testing principles.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. PREFERRED TECHNICAL SKILLS (BONUS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Familiarity or prior exposure to any of the following is considered an advantage:

1. NumPy: Vectorized computations, multidimensional arrays, and numerical operations.
2. Scikit-learn: Baseline knowledge of predictive modeling, feature scaling, and evaluation pipelines.
3. Machine Learning: Conceptual understanding of regression, classification, and clustering techniques.
4. Power BI: Experience building interactive visual dashboards, reports, and DAX calculations.
5. Tableau: Ability to craft visual stories, workbooks, and interactive charts.
6. Excel: Proficiency with pivot tables, advanced formulas (XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH), and financial models.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. EDUCATIONAL REQUIREMENTS & BACKGROUND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics,
  Information Systems, Engineering, Economics, or a related quantitative field.
- Equivalent practical experience demonstrated through substantial academic capstone projects,
  relevant technical internships, or a portfolio of open-source data analytics repositories.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. PROJECT & PORTFOLIO EXPECTATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Evidence of hands-on coursework, personal projects, or internship contributions involving
  real-world or synthetic data analysis workflows.
- Familiarity with version control (Git/GitHub) for collaborative development and code sharing.
- Strong problem-solving mindset, curiosity, and effective written communication skills.
